# Support and bug reports

Everything goes through the **[issue tracker](https://github.com/iri-dev/WardenOne/issues/new/choose)**. There are a few short forms to pick from:

- **🐛 Something broke?** A site stopped working, or a feature did something odd. Open a **Bug report**. The most useful things you can tell me are the **site address** and **which switch is involved**. If turning one protection off fixes it, please name that one.
- **💡 Got an idea?** A new protection, or something that could work better. Open a **Feature request**.
- **❓ Just a question?** Have a quick look at the [README](https://github.com/iri-dev/WardenOne#readme) first, since almost everything can be switched on or off from the popup. If it's still unclear, open an issue and ask me.

WardenOne runs on your own device, with no account and no telemetry, so I can't see anything that goes wrong for you. The issue tracker is how I find out, and I read every report 💜
