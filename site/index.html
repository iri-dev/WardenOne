WardenOne

One extension. Every defence.

WardenOne is a free, open-source privacy and security browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera. It folds ad blocking, tracker blocking, anti-fingerprinting, phishing and scam protection, download scanning and IP-leak protection into a single extension — with no account, no sign-up and no telemetry. Everything runs on your own device.

About downloads from anywhere else

WardenOne is published only from github.com/iri-dev/WardenOne, and it is a browser extension. It has never been distributed as an .exe, an installer or a setup program, and it never will be.

In August 2026 a copy of this project was republished elsewhere with its download links redirected to a file that was not WardenOne — a Windows executable that antivirus vendors classify as a trojan loader and browser credential stealer. That account has since been removed. If you ran a WardenOne "installer" obtained anywhere other than the link above, treat your browser data as exposed: change your passwords starting with email, turn on two-factor authentication, sign out of all sessions so stolen cookies stop working, and run a full antivirus scan.

What it does

  • Ad and content blocking — EasyList-style filtering, plus YouTube and Twitch video ads.
  • Tracker and analytics blocking — third-party cookies, first-party tracker catching, referrer trimming, De-AMP.
  • Anti-fingerprinting — canvas, WebGL, audio and hardware-hint noise, and detection of probing scripts.
  • Phishing and scam protection — look-alike domain blocking, login-page age checks, command-paste and tech-support-scam guards.
  • Download and file safety — download scanning and ad-gate skipping.
  • Privacy controls — WebRTC IP-leak protection, camera and microphone permission control, Global Privacy Control.
  • Script control — NoScript-style per-site blocking with a trusted allowlist.
  • Memory management — sleeps idle tabs without throwing away unsaved work.

Install

Download the latest release from the Releases page, unzip it, then open your browser's extensions page (chrome://extensions, edge://extensions, brave://extensions or opera://extensions), turn on Developer mode and choose Load unpacked.

Privacy

WardenOne has no servers of its own and no analytics. It does not track you and it does not send your browsing history anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
