Highlights

Three things you'll notice first.

01 · At the risky moment

A scam instruction should be interrupted, not silently logged.

A fake verification page may ask you to paste a command into your computer. WardenOne flags that ClickFix pattern on the page and explains why it is dangerous before you follow the instruction.

It also blocks known malicious and look-alike destinations through browser rules and checks selected deceptive page behaviour. New scams can be missed, and an unflagged page is not a safety verdict.

How WardenOne makes a decision {{icon:arrow-right}}

02 · Before a file continues

Pause and review a download that deserves a closer look.

Download Shield uses the source, file name, type, browser signals and local rules to assess the downloads Chrome reports. A suspicious case opens a review with the reasons and a choice to cancel or continue.

File Shield is a separate check that starts only when you choose a file. It examines the bytes locally; optional outside lookups are your choice.

See the data and provider choices {{icon:arrow-right}}

03 · After the decision

See the reason in a local activity record.

The Activity Centre groups recent blocks, warnings and exceptions. Protection Health shows whether core safeguards are running and whether a list or setting needs attention.

The record covers actions WardenOne observed or took. For request-level detail the Network Logger goes deeper, and an empty count does not prove a page made no risky requests.
